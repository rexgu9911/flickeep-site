How it works

One at a time.
Keep what matters.

No grids to scroll through, no folders to plan. flickeep shuffles your library and hands you one photo at a time, like a stack of prints on the table.

  1. 01

    Take a look.

    Open flickeep and your memories are there, shuffled. Or pick a month, your screenshots, or an album.

  2. 02

    Swipe right to keep.

    The prints you love stay right where they are. It’s as quick as it feels.

  3. 03

    Swipe left to let go.

    It comes apart into dots and waits in Trash. Nothing is deleted yet.

  4. 04

    Tap to turn it over.

    On the back: the day, the time of day and the place, written by hand. Share it from there, as a print.

Does swiping left delete my photo?

No. It waits in flickeep’s Trash. You look it over and confirm, and then your iPhone asks once more. Deleted photos stay in Photos › Recently Deleted for about 30 days. Favorites can’t be sent to Trash.

Does flickeep upload my photos?

Never. flickeep has no server and no account. Your library is read on your iPhone through Apple Photos. When you turn a print over, Apple’s map service is asked for the name of the place, using only the location, never the photo.

Does it work with iCloud Photos?

Yes. You choose whether flickeep may download photos from iCloud: on this iPhone only, over Wi-Fi, or over Wi-Fi and cellular. Deleting a photo, once you confirm, syncs to your other devices like it does in Photos.

What’s free, and what’s Pro?

flickeep is free to try: sort 20 photos and watch 10 videos. Looking back, albums, the calendar, sharing, Trash and deleting stay free. flickeep Pro removes the limit, and has no ads either.

Will it free up space?

Deleting photos can free space. Trash shows the size of the original files; the space your iPhone gets back can be different, especially with iCloud Photos.

How do I cancel or restore Pro?

Cancel in your iPhone’s Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel a subscription. On a new iPhone, open Settings in flickeep and tap Restore purchases.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone with iOS 18 or later.
