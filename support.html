How it works

Take a look opens your photos one at a time. Swipe right to keep, left to let go, up and down to look around. Tap a photo to turn it over. In Settings, “Take the tour again” shows the gestures once more.

Is anything deleted when I swipe left?

No. It waits in flickeep’s Trash. You review the list and confirm, and then Apple Photos asks once more. Deleted items stay in Photos › Recently Deleted for about 30 days. Favorites can’t be sent to Trash.

Free and Pro

flickeep is free to try: sort 20 photos and watch 10 videos. Looking back, albums, the calendar, sharing, Trash and deleting stay free. flickeep Pro removes the limit: weekly, yearly (with a free trial for new subscribers) or once for good. Prices are shown in the app in your currency.

Restoring or cancelling Pro

On a new iPhone, open Settings in flickeep and tap Restore purchases. To cancel a subscription, open the iPhone’s Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Photo access

flickeep only sees the photos you allow. To change it: iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › flickeep. Your photos never leave your iPhone; see the privacy policy.
